Patented process helps wastewater facilities overcome biological inhibition to process higher organic loads, improve stability and get more from existing infrastructure September 23, 2026 – Moleaer , the global leader in nanobubble technology, today announced a U.S. patent for a process that uses nanobubbles to reduce biological inhibition in anaerobic treatment, enabling facilities to process higher organic loads and increase biogas production with existing infrastructure.
