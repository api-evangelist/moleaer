Seafarming Systems selects Moleaer technology for new Aquatraz cages May 18, 2026 – Seafarming Systems has chosen Moleaer 's nanobubble technology as the oxygenation solution for the company's six closed Aquatraz cages, which are set to be completed at the end of 2026. The choice marks an important step forward in the development of sustainable and closed production solutions for the aquaculture industry.
