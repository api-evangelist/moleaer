Insights from Moleaer's Josh Bachner on Wash Talk: The Carwash Podcast As car wash operators face rising water costs, tighter regulations, and increasing pressure to improve wash quality, many are rethinking how water is treated and reused onsite. In a recent episode of Wash Talk: The Carwash Podcast , host Brian Ankney sat down with Josh Bachner, SVP of Marketing and CorporateDevelopment at Moleaer , to discuss how Moleaer nanobubble technology is helping car washes operate more efficiently, without major system overhauls.
