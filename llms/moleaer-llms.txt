# Moleaer

> Moleaer Inc. is a Hawthorne, California nanobubble technology company that designs and manufactures
> nanobubble generators used to raise dissolved oxygen, act as a chemical-free oxidant, and control algae
> and biofilm in aquaculture, horticulture and irrigation water, lakes and ponds, wastewater treatment,
> food and beverage, car washes, mining, and oil and gas. Moleaer publishes NO public API, SDK, developer
> portal or machine-readable specification. Its only digital surface is a customer-only remote monitoring
> service delivered through a login-gated portal.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- There is no public API, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/WSDL contract, and no `/.well-known` document
  on any Moleaer host. Probed 2026-08-26: `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
  `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`, `/graphql`, `/swagger/v1/swagger.json` all 404 on
  www.moleaer.com; api.moleaer.com, developer.moleaer.com and docs.moleaer.com do not resolve in DNS.
- Remote monitoring of equipment and water quality (DO, pH, ORP, temperature, conductivity) is a
  customer-only service. Its portal, www.monitoring-moleaer.com, is a Microsoft-IIS/ASP.NET application
  that 302-redirects every non-login path to `/login.aspx`. No public endpoint, no reference.
- Equipment-level integration is offered at the industrial-control layer on the generators themselves
  (SCADA, Modbus, EtherNet/IP, PROFINET per Moleaer's own controls-engineering job postings), not as a
  web API. Register maps are not published publicly.
- Everything below is human documentation and marketing content, not a callable surface.
